OJK Sandbox vs. PFII — Which Path Should You Take?

On July 21, 2026, Indonesia enacted UU PFII. Every RWA tokenization project now faces a critical choice: proceed via the proven OJK Sandbox (POJK 3/2024) or wait for the new PFII pathway (tax incentives, global flexibility). Arsiva is the only team that can analyze both and build your compliance system.

🏛️
OJK Sandbox (POJK 3/2024)

Proven, Operational, Limited Tax

The existing regulatory sandbox. Recognized as "Backed DFA" by OJK. Proven pathway with clear procedures — but limited tax incentives and less global structuring flexibility.

✓ Arsiva advantage: We've structured STO/RWA deals through OJK Sandbox. Our compliance OS automates reporting, audit trails, and legal structuring — reducing preparation time by up to 60%.
📜
UU PFII (New Pathway)

Tax Incentives, Global Flexibility — Pending Regs

The newly enacted UU PFII offers significant tax incentives and greater flexibility for cross-border structures. But implementing regulations are still pending — creating uncertainty for investors.

✓ Arsiva advantage: Once PFII implementing regulations are released, our Neo4j Knowledge Graph + FAISS RAG system will analyze trade-offs in real-time — recommending the optimal pathway with hard data, not guesswork.
