Certiva — Regulatory Retrieval Engine

Dubai Rental Rules corpus · Deterministic retrieval with article-level citations

Certiva retrieves the exact regulatory article that answers a question — and declines when it cannot. Hybrid retrieval (BM25 + semantic embeddings) is fused with Reciprocal Rank Fusion; every answer carries a source label and an article-level citation. When confidence falls below threshold, the engine returns no answer rather than a guess.
